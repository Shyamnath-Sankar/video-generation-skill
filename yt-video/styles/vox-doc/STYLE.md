# vox-doc: documentary explainer (Vox-inspired)

Serious, journalistic, tactile. Paper, print, highlighter and marker on real-looking documents.

## Look
- Warm paper `#F1EADB` with fibre texture and grain; ink `#1A1A1A`.
- Highlighter yellow `#FFE45C` swipes behind key phrases; red marker `#D7263D` circles, arrows and underlines (draw them with `K.draw`, slightly irregular paths); blue `#2E5BBA` for maps and data.
- Playfair Display 800 for headlines and big quotes, Geist for body/labels, JetBrains Mono for dates and sources.
- Documents as white cards with soft shadow and a source line at the bottom (`Source: …`): only real sources.
- Captions: `minimal`, 54 px.

## Motion
- `slide` transition: pages slide horizontally like turning a file.
- Highlighter grows left→right on the spoken phrase; marker circles draw on in 0.5 s; documents slide in and settle with a tiny rotation.
- Maps: country/region fills on its name, a pin drops, labels type in.

## Recipes
- **"Why X happened"**: headline card → document with the key line highlighted → chart (real data only) → map → closing quote.
- **Timeline**: a horizontal-then-vertical ruler with dates typing in, the camera tracks down the frame.

## Don't
- The Vox logo or brand name, fake newspaper mastheads, fabricated quotes or documents. Every document or quote shown must be real and attributed.
